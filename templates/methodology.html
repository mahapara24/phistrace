Method

How a docket is produced

A check runs input, preparation, a rule signal, retrieval, a separate verifier, and a report. The signal is a table of weights you can read. No classifier is trained.

  1. 1InputA single http or https URL, at most 2048 characters.
  2. 2PrepareHost, registrable domain, suffix, port, tokens, and a digit-for-letter fold.
  3. 3SignalSum of fired weights, capped at 100. Bands at 21, 50, and 75.
  4. 4RetrieveTag lookup in the agentic path, or one word-overlap search.
  5. 5AgentsFixed roles: detect, choose path, registries, retrieve, verify, report.
  6. 6DocketPublished sentences, withheld sentences, checklist, and guidance.

Step 3 · the signal

Weights, not a fitted model

No classifier is trained, and the site does not print accuracy, precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, or PR-AUC. Those numbers need a fitted model and a labeled test set. The score is the sum of the weights below.

Score = min(100, sum of fired weights). Low is 0–20. Moderate is 21–49. High is 50–74. Critical is 75–100.

Steps 4 and 5 · retrieval and agents

The next tool depends on the score

Light · below 21

Read the low-score exhibit. Retrieve any indicator that still fired. A brand token or a listed suffix can still open its registry.

Standard · 21 to 49

Retrieve one exhibit per fired indicator, using the indicator's tag. Do not open both registries unless a token or a listed suffix is present.

Deep · 50 and above

Open the brand registry and the suffix registry, then retrieve each fired indicator by tag. Verification still runs before the report.

Agents here are fixed functions with tools. They are not a generative model. The verifier is a different function from the writer. In the agentic mode the writer receives only sentences the verifier marked as covered. In the other two modes the writer publishes the candidate sentences anyway, which is why their unsupported-claim rate stays visible.

Metrics this deployment computes

Evidence, not a leaderboard

Unsupported-claim rate

Candidate sentences that lack a covering exhibit, divided by all candidate sentences. Withheld sentences still count. The agentic report can withhold them so the published pages stay covered.

Support in published findings

The share of published sentences that have a covering exhibit. Rules-only is 0 when it publishes a class label. Agentic stays at 100% when every published sentence is covered.

Investigation completeness

Required checks that actually ran, divided by the checks this score calls for. A mode is not graded against a shorter checklist of its own. The signal is held constant across the three modes.

Time

Retrieval time is the local search. Chain time is the whole mode. Both are measured on this machine. No network round trip is included, because no request is sent.

Boundaries

What the docket cannot claim

  • The page, the certificate, and the redirect target are unseen. A shortener stays a shortener.
  • The brand list and the suffix list are classroom lists, not a live threat feed.
  • A low score is a reason to keep the investigation short. It is not permission to type a password.
  • A person who controls a URL can change its shape. These rules are a first signal for a docket, not a control to deploy alone.
  • The data page scores PhiUSIIL, an OpenPhish snapshot, and the Phishing Database lists. It reports how often these fixed rules fire. It does not train on those files.
