Problem statement

Detection is the first signal. It is not the investigation.

Phishing URLs and websites are still treated as a binary classification task. A model score can flag a known pattern. The score does not open a brand list, does not attach a reason a person can check, and does not keep unsupported sentences out of the write-up.

Retrieval can add cybersecurity context. One retrieve-and-answer step still uses a single query. It cannot choose the next source from the score, and it can attach a paragraph that does not cover the claim.

The investigation needs a controlled sequence. The next check depends on the score. URL features, the brand list, the suffix list, and the knowledge base are separate tools. Verification stays separate from report writing, so the docket cannot cite a sentence the exhibits do not cover.
